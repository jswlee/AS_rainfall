# Job Description: Climate Data Science & Machine Learning Specialist

## DUTIES

**Essential functions of this position:**

• **Climate Data Pipeline Development & Management:** Design, implement, and maintain comprehensive data preprocessing pipelines for multi-source environmental datasets to support machine learning model development and operational forecasting systems

• **Advanced Machine Learning Model Development:** Research, design, and optimize deep neural network architectures using PyTorch or TensorFlow for environmental prediction tasks, including hyperparameter tuning, cross-validation, ensemble methods, and performance optimization to achieve production-ready model accuracy

• **Research Documentation & Technical Communication:** Author comprehensive technical documentation, develop reproducible analytical workflows, and deliver presentations to interdisciplinary research teams and external stakeholders to ensure project continuity

• **Statistical Analysis & Model Validation:** Implement rigorous statistical modeling approaches, cross-validation frameworks, and performance evaluation metrics to ensure model reliability, interpretability, and operational readiness for deployment

• **Software Development & System Integration:** Develop and maintain scalable Python-based applications, RESTful APIs, and database systems to support research operations, data management, and model deployment infrastructure

• **Collaborative Research & Project Management:** Collaborate with interdisciplinary teams including hydrologists, climatologists, and policy researchers to translate scientific requirements into technical solutions while managing project timelines and deliverables

• **Data Quality Assurance & Governance:** Establish and maintain data validation protocols, quality control procedures, and documentation standards to ensure data integrity and reproducibility across research projects

## ANTICIPATED PRIMARY QUALIFICATIONS

**Minimum requirements to successfully carry out the duties described:**

### Education

• **Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Statistics, Environmental Science, or related quantitative field** with demonstrated project work in machine learning, statistics, and programming

• **Master's degree preferred** with thesis or research experience in machine learning, data science, or environmental modeling applications

### Technical Skills

• **Advanced proficiency in Python programming** including scientific computing libraries (NumPy, Pandas, SciPy), machine learning frameworks (PyTorch, TensorFlow, Scikit-learn), and data visualization tools (Matplotlib, Seaborn)

• **Deep learning and machine learning expertise** including neural network architecture design, hyperparameter optimization, model evaluation, ensemble methods, and statistical modeling techniques

• **Database management and SQL proficiency** with experience in data pipeline development, ETL processes, and working with large-scale environmental datasets

• **Version control and software development practices** using Git, collaborative development workflows, and documentation standards

### Domain Knowledge

• **Geospatial analysis experience** including working with geographic information systems (GIS), spatial data formats, and coordinate reference systems

• **Statistical analysis and research methodology** including experimental design, hypothesis testing, cross-validation, and performance evaluation metrics

• **Understanding of environmental or climate data** including familiarity with meteorological variables, time series analysis, and data quality challenges in environmental datasets

### Communication & Collaboration

• **Technical writing and documentation skills** with ability to produce clear, comprehensive documentation for complex analytical workflows and model development processes

• **Presentation and stakeholder communication abilities** including experience presenting technical findings to both technical and non-technical audiences

• **Collaborative research experience** working effectively in interdisciplinary teams and managing multiple project priorities

### Preferred Qualifications

• **Experience with cloud computing platforms** and containerization technologies (Docker) for scalable model deployment

• **Publications or presentations** in peer-reviewed venues demonstrating research contribution and technical communication skills

• **Multilingual capabilities** particularly in languages relevant to Pacific region research collaborations

• **Experience with real-time data systems** and operational forecasting environments

• **Familiarity with climate modeling** or hydrological forecasting applications
